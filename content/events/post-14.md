This workshop provides comprehensive training on research methodologies used in social sciences, including survey design, qualitative research methods, and data analysis techniques.

## Workshop Content

- Survey design and implementation
- Interview techniques
- Focus group facilitation
- Qualitative data analysis
- Ethical considerations in social research

## Target Audience

Graduate students and early-career researchers in social sciences and humanities.
