Essential training for all researchers on ethical conduct in research, including topics on data integrity, authorship, conflict of interest, and research misconduct.

## Training Modules

- Research ethics principles
- Data management and integrity
- Authorship and publication ethics
- Conflict of interest management
- Research misconduct prevention

## Certification

This training is required for all researchers and provides certification upon completion.
