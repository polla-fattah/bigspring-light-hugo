## Laboratory Overview

The Automation and Robotic Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in industrial automation, robotics, and control systems.

## Purpose

This lab supports automation and robotic lab experiments covering:
- Industrial robotics
- Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs)
- Automation systems
- Control systems
- Human-machine interfaces

## Facilities

- Robotic systems
- PLC training stations
- Automation workstations
- Control panels
- Programming interfaces

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must complete robotics and automation safety training before accessing this facility. Safety protocols must be followed when working with robotic systems.
