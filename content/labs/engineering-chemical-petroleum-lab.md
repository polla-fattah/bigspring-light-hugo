## Laboratory Overview

The Chemical and Petroleum Engineering Lab is part of the Chemical and Petroleum Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students and researchers with access to advanced chemical analysis instruments, reactors, and processing equipment for chemical engineering and petroleum engineering experiments.

## Purpose

This lab supports experiments and research in:
- Petroleum analysis and testing
- Chemical reaction engineering
- Separation processes
- Material characterization
- Process instrumentation
- Chemical synthesis

## Research Areas

- Petroleum refining processes
- Chemical reaction kinetics
- Separation and purification
- Material analysis and characterization
- Process optimization
- Safety and environmental engineering

## Facilities

- Analytical instruments (FT-IR, flash point tester)
- Reactors (high pressure, glass reactor)
- Separation equipment (centrifuge, rotary evaporator)
- Heating and mixing equipment
- Safety equipment (fume hoods)
- Water purification system

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab coordinator at chemical-petroleum-lab@su.edu.krd.

## Safety

All users must complete chemical safety training before accessing this facility. Proper personal protective equipment (PPE) must be worn at all times. Safety protocols for handling chemicals, pressurized equipment, and hazardous materials must be strictly followed.
