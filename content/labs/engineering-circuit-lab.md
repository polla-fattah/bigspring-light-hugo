## Laboratory Overview

The Circuit Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in circuit analysis, design, and testing.

## Purpose

This lab supports circuit lab experiments covering:
- Basic circuit analysis
- AC and DC circuit design
- Measurement techniques
- Circuit troubleshooting
- Electronic component testing

## Facilities

- Circuit training stations
- Measurement instruments
- Component storage
- Workbenches for circuit assembly

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must follow electrical safety protocols when working with circuit equipment.
