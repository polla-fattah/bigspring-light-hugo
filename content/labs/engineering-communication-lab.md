## Laboratory Overview

The Communication Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in communication systems, signal processing, and RF engineering.

## Purpose

This lab supports communication lab experiments covering:
- Analog and digital communication
- Modulation and demodulation
- Signal processing
- RF systems
- Antenna systems

## Facilities

- Communication training stations
- Signal generators and analyzers
- RF test equipment
- Antenna measurement setup
- Network analyzers

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must follow RF safety protocols when working with communication equipment. Proper handling of RF equipment is essential.
