## Laboratory Overview

The Electronic Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in electronic circuit design, analysis, and testing.

## Purpose

This lab supports electronic lab experiments covering:
- Electronic circuit design
- Signal processing
- Amplifier design
- Filter circuits
- Electronic component characterization

## Facilities

- Electronic training stations
- Signal generators
- Test and measurement equipment
- Component inventory

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must follow electrical safety protocols when working with electronic equipment.
