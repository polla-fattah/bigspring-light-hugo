## Laboratory Overview

The GIS Lab is part of the Geomatics Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with access to computer workstations equipped with Geographic Information Systems (GIS) software for spatial data analysis, mapping, and geospatial modeling.

## Purpose

This lab supports GIS practical training covering:
- Spatial data analysis
- Digital mapping
- Geodatabase management
- Spatial modeling
- Cartographic design

## Facilities

- 17 All-in-one desktop computers
- GIS software platforms
- Spatial analysis tools
- Data storage and management systems

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisors.

## Software

- ArcGIS Desktop/Pro
- QGIS
- Other GIS software platforms
- Spatial analysis extensions
