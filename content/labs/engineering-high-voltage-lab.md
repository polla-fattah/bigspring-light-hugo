## Laboratory Overview

The High Voltage Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in high-voltage engineering, insulation testing, and electrical breakdown analysis.

## Purpose

This lab supports high voltage lab experiments covering:
- High-voltage testing
- Insulation testing
- Breakdown voltage analysis
- Dielectric strength testing
- High-voltage safety

## Facilities

- High-voltage test equipment
- Insulation testing stations
- Safety enclosures
- Emergency shut-off systems
- Personal protective equipment

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must complete comprehensive high-voltage safety training before accessing this facility. This lab requires strict adherence to high-voltage safety protocols and personal protective equipment.
