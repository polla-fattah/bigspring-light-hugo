## Laboratory Overview

The Machine Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in electrical machines, including motors, generators, and transformers.

## Purpose

This lab supports machine lab experiments covering:
- DC machines (motors and generators)
- AC machines (induction and synchronous)
- Transformer characteristics
- Machine control systems
- Performance analysis

## Facilities

- Motor-generator sets
- Machine training stations
- Load testing equipment
- Control panels
- Safety equipment

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must complete machine safety training before accessing this facility. Proper safety protocols must be followed when working with rotating machinery.
