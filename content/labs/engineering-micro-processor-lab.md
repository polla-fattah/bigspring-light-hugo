## Laboratory Overview

The Micro Processor Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students and researchers with hands-on experience in microprocessor systems, embedded programming, and digital electronics.

## Purpose

This lab supports micro processor lab experiments covering:
- Microprocessor architecture and programming
- Embedded systems development
- Digital circuit design
- Interface programming
- System integration

## Facilities

- Microprocessor development kits
- Programming interfaces
- Testing and debugging equipment
- Workbenches for circuit assembly

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must follow electrical safety protocols when working with microprocessor equipment.
