## Laboratory Overview

The Photogrammetry Lab is part of the Geomatics Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students and researchers with access to high-performance computing resources for photogrammetric data processing, 3D modeling, and aerial image analysis.

## Purpose

This lab supports photogrammetry practical training and research covering:
- Aerial photogrammetry
- Digital photogrammetry
- 3D modeling from images
- Stereo image processing
- Point cloud generation
- Orthophoto production

## Facilities

- 10 High-performance workstations
- Dedicated server for data processing
- Photogrammetry software
- Large-format digitizers (if available)
- 3D visualization systems

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

- Photogrammetry processing software
- 3D modeling applications
