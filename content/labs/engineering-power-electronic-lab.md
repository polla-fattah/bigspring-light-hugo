## Laboratory Overview

The Power Electronic Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in power electronics, including converters, inverters, and power control systems.

## Purpose

This lab supports power electronic lab experiments covering:
- Power converter design
- Inverter circuits
- Motor drives
- Power control systems
- Switching power supplies

## Facilities

- Power electronic training stations
- High-power test equipment
- Load banks
- Safety equipment for high-voltage work

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must complete high-voltage safety training before accessing this facility. Strict safety protocols must be followed when working with power electronic equipment.
