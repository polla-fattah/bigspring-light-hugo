## Laboratory Overview

The Power Lab is part of the Electrical Engineering Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students with hands-on experience in power systems, protection, and analysis.

## Purpose

This lab supports power lab experiments covering:
- Power system analysis
- Protection systems
- Fault analysis
- Power quality
- Load flow studies

## Facilities

- Power system training stations
- Protection equipment
- Measurement instruments
- Load banks
- Safety equipment

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisor.

## Safety

All users must complete high-voltage power systems safety training before accessing this facility. Strict safety protocols must be followed when working with power systems.
