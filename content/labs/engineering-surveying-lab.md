## Laboratory Overview

The Surveying Lab is part of the Geomatics Department at the College of Engineering. This laboratory provides students and researchers with access to a comprehensive range of surveying instruments for field surveying practical training, research, and professional development.

## Purpose

This lab supports surveying practical training and research covering:
- Land surveying techniques
- Topographic surveying
- Construction surveying
- Cadastral surveying
- Hydrographic surveying
- High-precision measurements

## Facilities

- Total stations (TOPCON, LEICA)
- Digital theodolites
- Automatic levels (WILD, LEICA)
- Digital levels
- GNSS/GPS receivers
- Laser scanner (3D scanning)
- Echosounder (hydrographic surveying)

## Equipment Status

**Working Equipment:**
- Multiple total stations (TOPCON GTS-235N, LEICA TS03)
- Digital theodolites (KOLIDA, SOUTH DT-2, TOPCON DT-205)
- Automatic levels (WILD NO1, WILD NAK-0, LEICA NA720)
- Digital levels (LEICA DNA10)
- GNSS receivers (LEICA GS16)
- Laser scanner (LEICA Scan Station2)
- Echosounder

**Out of Service:**

- 2 SOUTH DT-2 digital theodolites (Serial: 15487, 15573)
- 1 WILD NAK-0 automatic level (Serial: 389122)
- 1 LEICA NA720 automatic level (Serial: 5624025)
- 2 LEICA GNSS1200 GNSS receivers (Serial: 09020046, 09020022)

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab supervisors. Field equipment may require advance booking for field exercises.

## Field Equipment

Surveying instruments are primarily used for field exercises. Students are responsible for proper handling, transportation, and return of equipment.

## Safety

All users must complete surveying equipment safety training before accessing instruments. Proper handling procedures must be followed to prevent damage to precision instruments.
