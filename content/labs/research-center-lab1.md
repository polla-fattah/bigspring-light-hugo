## Laboratory Overview

Lab 1 is a general-purpose research laboratory located at the Research Center. This facility provides researchers with access to analytical instruments, biological equipment, and safety infrastructure to support a wide range of research activities.

## Research Areas

- Analytical chemistry
- Molecular biology
- Protein analysis
- Sample preparation
- Laboratory safety

## Facilities

- Fume hoods for chemical safety
- Analytical instrumentation
- Biological equipment
- Sample preparation areas
- Storage facilities

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab coordinator at lab1@su.edu.krd.

## Safety

All users must complete safety training before accessing this facility. Safety guidelines and protocols are available in the regulations section.
