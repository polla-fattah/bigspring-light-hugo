## Laboratory Overview

Lab 2 is a general-purpose research laboratory located at the Research Center. This facility provides researchers with access to advanced analytical instruments, biological equipment, and environmental controls to support a wide range of research activities.

## Research Areas

- Cell biology
- Molecular biology
- Analytical chemistry
- Sample processing
- Temperature-controlled experiments

## Facilities

- Temperature-controlled environments
- Centrifugation equipment
- Microscopy facilities
- Spectroscopic instruments
- Biological sample storage

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab coordinator at lab2@su.edu.krd.

## Safety

All users must complete safety training before accessing this facility. Safety guidelines and protocols are available in the regulations section.
