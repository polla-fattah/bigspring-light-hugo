## Laboratory Overview

Lab 4 is a general-purpose research laboratory located at the Research Center. This facility provides researchers with access to analytical instruments, biological equipment, and specialized storage facilities to support a wide range of research activities.

## Research Areas

- Sample storage and preservation
- Analytical chemistry
- High-throughput screening
- Chemical safety
- Sample processing

## Facilities

- Ultra-low temperature storage
- Fume hoods for chemical safety
- Analytical instrumentation
- Sample preparation areas
- Plate reading capabilities

## Access and Booking

For access to this laboratory or specific equipment, please contact the lab coordinator at lab4@su.edu.krd.

## Safety

All users must complete safety training before accessing this facility. Safety guidelines and protocols are available in the regulations section.
