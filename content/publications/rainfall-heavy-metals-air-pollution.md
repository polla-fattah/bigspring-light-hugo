## Abstract

This environmental monitoring study assessed heavy metal contamination in rainfall near the Erbil Steel Factory, using atmospheric deposition as an indicator of air pollution. The research compared heavy metal concentrations between the industrial site and a rural control area to evaluate environmental impact.

## Methodology

Rainwater samples were collected from sites near the Erbil Steel Factory and compared with samples from a rural control location. Heavy metal analysis was performed for Mn, Pb, Fe, As, Co, Se, Hg, and Cd. Geo-accumulation index and pollution load index were calculated to assess contamination levels.

## Key Findings

The study revealed significant contamination:

- **Heavy Metal Levels**: Concentrations of Mn, Pb, Fe, As, Co, Se, Hg, and Cd were significantly higher near the factory compared to control sites
- **Geo-accumulation Index**: Mn showed an index of 6.28, indicating extreme contamination
- **Pollution Load Index**: The overall pollution load index of 13.46 demonstrates heavy metal pollution in the area
- **Environmental Impact**: Atmospheric deposition contributes significantly to environmental contamination

## Implications

These findings have important implications for:

- Environmental monitoring and pollution control strategies
- Public health protection in industrial areas
- Industrial emissions management and regulation

## Conclusion

The study demonstrates significant heavy metal contamination in rainfall near the Erbil Steel Factory, highlighting the need for improved air pollution control measures and continued environmental monitoring to protect public health and ecosystem integrity.
