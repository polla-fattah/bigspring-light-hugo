## Abstract

This quantitative study analyzed genetic and non-genetic factors affecting milk production in Saanen goats in the Kurdistan Region. The research examined heritability estimates and environmental influences on productive traits, providing insights for breeding and management strategies.

## Methodology

The study employed quantitative genetic analysis to evaluate heritability estimates and environmental factors. Performance data from Saanen goats in the Kurdistan Region were analyzed using statistical models to separate genetic and environmental effects.

## Key Findings

The research revealed significant findings:

- **Heritability Estimates**: Quantitative analysis of genetic contributions to milk production traits
- **Environmental Factors**: Identification of non-genetic factors affecting productive performance
- **Trait Correlations**: Relationships between different productive traits in Saanen goats
- **Breeding Implications**: Practical applications for selection and breeding programs

## Implications

These findings have practical applications for:

- Breeding programs targeting improved milk production
- Management strategies optimizing environmental conditions
- Genetic improvement in Saanen goat populations

## Conclusion

The study provides valuable insights into genetic and environmental factors affecting milk production in Saanen goats, offering practical guidance for livestock improvement programs in the Kurdistan Region.
