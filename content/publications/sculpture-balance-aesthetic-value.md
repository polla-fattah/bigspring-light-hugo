## Abstract

This theoretical study examines the concept of balance as an aesthetic value in contemporary sculpture, particularly in the context of regional Kurdish artistic practice. The research explores balance not merely as a physical requirement but as a fundamental philosophical and aesthetic principle in sculpture.

## Methodology

The study employed theoretical analysis and art criticism methods to examine balance in contemporary Kurdish sculpture. Artistic works were analyzed for their use of balance as both physical structure and aesthetic value, with consideration of cultural and philosophical contexts.

## Key Findings

The analysis revealed important insights:

- **Aesthetic Principle**: Balance as a core philosophical element in sculpture beyond physical necessity
- **Cultural Context**: How balance functions within Kurdish artistic traditions
- **Philosophical Dimensions**: Theoretical exploration of balance as an aesthetic value
- **Contemporary Practice**: Examination of balance in modern Kurdish sculpture

## Implications

These findings contribute to:

- Understanding aesthetic theory in regional art practice
- Art criticism and interpretation of Kurdish sculpture
- Theoretical approaches to balance in contemporary art

## Conclusion

The study demonstrates the importance of balance as both a physical and philosophical element in contemporary Kurdish sculpture, providing valuable insights into aesthetic values and artistic practice in the region.
