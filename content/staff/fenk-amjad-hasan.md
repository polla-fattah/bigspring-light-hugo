  Fenk Amjad Hasan works at the Scientific Research Center of Salahaddin University‑Erbil. Her research interests lie in psychology and education, particularly in how parental treatment influences adolescent mental health.
  
  In a field study of social phobia among secondary‑school students in Erbil, Hasan and her colleague Jalal Ahmed Abdullah surveyed 402 students and found that levels of social phobia were generally low and that parenting styles were predominantly democratic. The study concluded there were no significant differences in social phobia based on sex, educational level or type of school.
  
  She continues to explore topics such as adolescent behavior, parental influence and social anxiety, providing data to inform mental‑health interventions in Kurdistan.
