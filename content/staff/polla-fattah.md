  Dr Polla Fattah is a lecturer in the Department of Software Engineering at Salahaddin University‑Erbil and an active researcher in artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning and data mining. On his academic CV he states that he aims to harness computation to better understand thinking and information processing and to develop algorithms for automating data extraction. His goal is to use these technologies to solve complex problems and advance his society's understanding of science.

  Fattah's publications include deep‑learning approaches for age and gender estimation from skeletal radiographs and Kurdish handwritten‑character recognition, as well as research on machine‑translation evaluation metrics and domain adaptation. He teaches courses such as distributed databases, advanced programming and data mining, and has received multiple letters of appreciation for excellence in teaching and research.
