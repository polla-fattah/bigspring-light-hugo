  Dr Salim Omar Raoof is a Doctor of Animal Breeding and Quality Assurance Manager at Salahaddin University‑Erbil. His skills and expertise include animal production, breeding and genetics, cattle breeding, animal biotechnology and ruminant nutrition.
  
  Raoof's work focuses on improving livestock performance through selection and breeding strategies; he has published studies on milk production in goats, genetic and non‑genetic factors affecting Holstein cows, and quality management in animal production systems.
  
  In addition to research, Raoof teaches courses in animal science and provides training to farmers and students on genetics and breeding practices. He participates in committees responsible for evaluating animal production programs and works to enhance quality assurance in university laboratories and farms. His combination of practical breeding experience and academic expertise makes him a key figure in the region's livestock industry.
