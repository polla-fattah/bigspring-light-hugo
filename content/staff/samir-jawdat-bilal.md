  Samir Jawdat Bilal is an assistant professor of fish parasitology in the College of Agriculture at Salahaddin University‑Erbil. His ResearchGate biography notes that he teaches and supervises in the biology department and that his research interests centre on fish diseases and parasitology, ichthyology, ecology and fisheries science.
  
  He has led a project titled Review of Iraqi fish parasitic fauna, which examines parasites that affect fish in Iraqi waters, and he regularly publishes on topics such as fish biology, fisheries management and the reproductive biology of fish.
  
  Bilal's expertise extends to fish ecology and environmental monitoring. His research list includes studies on the prevalence of various protozoan and helminth parasites in fish and on the impacts of water quality on fish health. Through these projects he contributes to improving fishery management and disease control strategies in Iraq. As an academic he also mentors graduate students and collaborates with regional fisheries organizations to address aquatic animal health issues.
