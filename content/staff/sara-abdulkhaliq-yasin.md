  Sara Abdulkhaliq Yasin holds a Master's degree in phycolimnology and works in the Department of Environmental Sciences at Salahaddin University‑Erbil. Her ResearchGate profile lists skills in environmental science, algae and water analysis, and shows that she studies heavy‑metal exposure and oxidative stress in humans.
  
  Recent publications evaluate the impact of heavy metals on gasoline‑station workers, linking metal exposure to cardiovascular and respiratory stress, and assess the levels of contaminants in groundwater and tap water.
  
  Yasin also contributes to water‑quality assessments and ecological monitoring. Her work combines biomonitoring of trace metals with measurements of oxidative‑stress biomarkers in human blood, helping to identify health risks associated with environmental pollution.
