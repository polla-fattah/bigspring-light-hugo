  Sarbast Muhsen Azeez is affiliated with the Sculpture Division of the Department of Fine Arts at Salahaddin University's Scientific Research Centre. In a 2025 article he examined the concept of balance as an aesthetic value in contemporary sculpture in the Kurdistan Region.
  
  The study analysed types of balance—symmetrical, asymmetrical and illusory—and showed that Kurdish sculptors use balance to harmonize masses and spaces and achieve visual stability. The research concludes that balance is a fundamental aesthetic principle in contemporary Kurdish sculpture.
