  Shaho Jangi Noori is a researcher interested in water‑resource management and rainwater harvesting. In a review article co‑authored with Redvan Ghasemlounia, he notes that rainwater harvesting has gained renewed importance in arid and semi‑arid regions since the 1970s.
  
  The paper highlights that selecting suitable sites using remote sensing and GIS techniques is essential for maximizing harvested water and reducing ecological impacts. It summarizes criteria from previous studies, including climate, soil, topography and land use, for assessing potential rainwater‑harvesting sites.
  
  Noori's work emphasizes the integration of geographic information systems (GIS) and multi‑criteria decision analysis for site selection. His contributions help guide water‑resource planners and engineers in designing efficient harvesting systems, especially in regions facing water shortages.
