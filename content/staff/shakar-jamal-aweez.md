  Shakar Jamal Aweez is affiliated with the Scientific Research Centre at Salahaddin University‑Erbil. He participated in an environmental study that assessed heavy‑metal contamination of soils near the Khabat and Bazian oil‑refinery sites in Iraq.
  
  The researchers found elevated concentrations of chromium, nickel and cadmium in the soil and concluded that these metals pose a serious environmental and health threat. The team tested green nanoscale zero‑valent iron (nZVI) particles and observed that they effectively reduced heavy‑metal concentrations in polluted soils.
  
  Aweez's research interests therefore encompass soil pollution, environmental remediation and the application of nanotechnology to treat contaminated sites. His work contributes to sustainable environmental management and demonstrates how novel materials can mitigate industrial pollution.
