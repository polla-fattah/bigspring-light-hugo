  Suhad Asad Mustafa heads the molecular genetics laboratory at the General Directorate of Scientific Research Center at Salahaddin University‑Erbil. She holds a BSc and MSc in biology from the same university and obtained a PhD in molecular genetics from the University of Wales (United Kingdom).
  
  Mustafa's profile lists expertise in microRNA, biosensors, genotyping, enzymes, mutation analysis, cancer genomics and expression profiling.
  
  Her research group investigates gene expression patterns and molecular markers in cancer and infectious diseases. She collaborates on projects developing biosensors for pathogen detection and leads training workshops on molecular techniques. As a keynote speaker she promotes scientific research in Kurdistan and mentors postgraduate students in genetics and biotechnology.
