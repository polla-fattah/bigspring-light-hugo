  Treska Salih Hassan is a researcher from Salahaddin University‑Erbil whose work bridges molecular biology and cancer research. She co‑authored a paper showing that activation of liver X receptor (LXR) inhibits proliferation of human breast‑cancer cells by suppressing the PI3K–Akt signalling pathway.
  
  The study reported that LXR activation reduced phosphorylation of key kinases in the pathway and induced tumour‑suppressor phosphatases such as PTEN and PHLPP. By demonstrating that LXR ligands impair breast‑cancer cell growth and tumour formation in mice, the research suggests LXR agonists as potential anti‑cancer agents.
  
  Hassan's research interests therefore include cancer biology, molecular signalling and the development of therapeutic agents that modulate nuclear receptors. Her contributions add to the understanding of how metabolic signalling pathways can be targeted in cancer therapy.
