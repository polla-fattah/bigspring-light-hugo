# Evaluation of ChatGPT Generic Research Center Requirements

This document evaluates the generic requirements generated by ChatGPT against the actual data, scope, and technical realities of the **Salahaddin University Research Center (SURC)** Next.js migration project.

---

## 1. What is GOOD & Highly Relevant (Keep)

These requirements align perfectly with the SURC codebase, user requests, and Next.js/PostgreSQL architecture:

### A. Academic Identity & Brand Alignment (Sections 1 & 7)
*   **Why it's good:** Reinforces SUE's official Crimson/Maroon (`#C41E3A`) and Slate Blue (`#0B2545`) palette. It anchors the portal's design to the main university portal ([su.edu.krd](https://su.edu.krd)) while retaining a professional academic layout.
*   **Action:** Keep. We have already integrated these guidelines into the design parameters.

### B. Decoupled Core Directories (Sections 3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 3.7, 3.12)
*   **Why it's good:** Validates our relational PostgreSQL design for:
    *   **Staff/Researchers:** Profile grids with academic titles, bios, and linked entities.
    *   **Projects:** Filtering by status (ongoing/completed), year, and research unit.
    *   **Publications:** Structured list of articles, theses (MSc/PhD), and reports.
    *   **Labs & Equipment:** Standard directory lists and specific equipment status tracking.
*   **Action:** Keep. Our database schema is already modeled to resolve these relationships.

### C. Equipment Scheduling & Reservations (Section 3.12)
*   **Why it's good:** Directly supports the requested workflow where students/staff can request bookings, lab technicians (`ROLE_LAB_STAFF`) approve/reject them, and managers see utilization reports.
*   **Action:** Keep. Already integrated into our `equipment_reservations` table and dashboard specifications.

### D. Multi-lingual Right-to-Left (RTL) Support (Section 6)
*   **Why it's good:** SURC operates in the Kurdistan Region; hence, full support for English, Kurdish Sorani, and Arabic with proper RTL styling (using CSS logical properties) and clean font stacks (e.g., Noto Sans Arabic) is mandatory.
*   **Action:** Keep.

### E. Resources, Templates, and Regulations (Section 3.14)
*   **Why it's good:** Matches the actual files parsed from the Hugo site (`content/regulations/`, `content/templates/`). Moving these from static files to PostgreSQL tables (`regulations` and `forms`) will make updates easy.
*   **Action:** Keep.

## 2. What is TOO MUCH / Over-engineered (Discard or Simplify)

These requirements add unnecessary complexity, administrative overhead, or out-of-scope development time for a local research center:

### A. Generic "Research Areas" CRUD (Section 3.3)
*   **Why it's too much:** ChatGPT proposes a dynamic, code-free "Research Areas" administration console. For SURC, research areas are legally defined by the **8 Research Units** (e.g., Data Analysis Unit, Environmental Studies, etc.). Units represent the official structure and do not change dynamically.
*   **Recommendation:** Discard. Use the existing **Research Units** schema as the primary categorizer.

### B. Complex News blogging System (Section 3.8)
*   **Why it's too much:** Requirements like "scheduled publishing", "author tracking", and "content revision history" for news updates are overkill.
*   **Recommendation:** Simplify. Roll any news or announcements into the **Events** system (e.g., a "News/Announcements" category inside the `events` table) to keep administrative work simple.

### C. Funding & Opportunities Portal (Section 3.10)
*   **Why it's too much:** Building a searchable career/grant application tracking system (handling scholarships, internships, fellowships, and eligible deadlines) is too complex for this phase.
*   **Recommendation:** Simplify. Post open opportunities as basic document downloads in the **Forms & Templates** section, or as posts under **Events/Announcements**.

### D. Consulting Services CRUD Directory (Section 3.11)
*   **Why it's too much:** A dynamic checkout or fee-processing portal for proofreading services, plagiarism checks, and methodology consultation does not match SURC's academic workflow.
*   **Recommendation:** Discard. Display a static text block describing available trainings and tutorials (mapping to the existing `trainings-tutorials.md` template), with contact emails.

### E. Advanced External Integrations (Section 8)
*   **Why it's too much:** Automated publication imports from BibTeX uploads, direct sync with Crossref/Scopus APIs using DOI keys, and integration with SUE library search engines are high-maintenance and expensive to build.
*   **Recommendation:** Simplify. For Phase 1, researchers will link their publications manually inside their dashboard and paste external links (ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate) directly onto their profile pages as simple text URLs.

### F. WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility Auditing (Section 10)
*   **Why it's too much:** Complete keyboard navigation focus trap validation, screen-reader audio transcript generation, and accessibility compliance monitoring are over-engineered.
*   **Recommendation:** Simplify. Ensure clean semantic HTML tags, dynamic alt-text fields for uploaded images, and high color contrasts, without performing full formal WCAG audits.
